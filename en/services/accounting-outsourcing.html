Who this service is for

Suitable for companies that want a full-fledged accounting department without the cost of in-house staff — from a sole proprietor with a few transactions a month to a chain of retail outlets.

What's included

  • Bookkeeping and tax accounting
  • Preparing and filing mandatory reports on time
  • Calculating and optimizing tax payments
  • Bank-client work and e-invoice (ESF) preparation
  • Preparing reconciliation statements with counterparties

How the work is organized

After the contract is signed, the team gets to know your business structure, sets up or reviews your current accounting and takes over the whole cycle — from posting documents to filing reports.
