Accounting restoration

We'll put a neglected accounting function in order — from sorting through source documents to filing correct reports for past periods.

Discuss cooperationMessage us on Telegram

What's included

  • Analyzing the current state of accounting and identifying errors
  • Restoring source documents and ledgers
  • Recalculating tax liabilities for past periods
  • Preparing and filing amended tax returns
  • Resolving discrepancies with counterparties and the tax authority

Why it matters

Neglected accounting increases the risk of fines in an audit and prevents you from seeing the company's financial position objectively — the sooner restoration begins, the fewer corrections will be needed.

Frequently asked questions

For what period can accounting be restored?

Usually for the last 3–5 years, depending on how well the source documents have been preserved.

Do we need to stop current operations during the restoration?

No, restoration runs in parallel with your current accounting and does not affect the company's operations.

What if some documents are lost?

We'll help recover them through counterparties, the bank and the tax authorities, or propose alternative ways to confirm transactions.
